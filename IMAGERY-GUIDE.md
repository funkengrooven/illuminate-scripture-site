# Free Imagery Resources for Bible Apps Website

## Best Free Stock Photo Sources

### 1. **Unsplash** (unsplash.com)
- **Best for**: Modern, high-quality photos of people reading, studying, technology
- **Recommended searches**: 
  - "person reading bible"
  - "diverse people studying"
  - "accessibility technology"
  - "modern workspace"
  - "hands holding book"
  - "peaceful reading"

### 2. **Pexels** (pexels.com)
- **Best for**: Professional photos with good diversity representation
- **Recommended searches**:
  - "bible study group"
  - "inclusive technology"
  - "diverse students"
  - "reading together"
  - "assistive technology"

### 3. **Pixabay** (pixabay.com)
- **Best for**: Bible-specific imagery, illustrations
- **Recommended searches**:
  - "bible study"
  - "scripture reading"
  - "open bible"
  - "peaceful study"

### 4. **Freepik** (freepik.com - with attribution)
- **Best for**: Modern illustrations, icons, vector graphics
- **Great for**: Hero section backgrounds, decorative elements
